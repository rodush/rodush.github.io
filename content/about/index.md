# A bit of history

Hi, I am Roman Dushko.

I was born long time ago - in 1984 (same year as the title of the novel by George Orwell).

Originally I am from Ukraine. Moved to the Netherlands in 2015, where I live until now.

# Professional life

My professional carreer started around 2005, when I learned to create static websites with PHP4, MySQL, CSS, HTML4, and vanilla JavaScript. They were running on LAMP stack.

Through the years I have built many websites and platforms of different complexities.\
The most challenging project I would consider so far was DAZN (www.dazn.com) - Platform for live streaming of sport events around the Globe. The challenge there was to support multi-million userbase, providing fast and resilient response time from the APIs.

Currently I am working as an Engineering Manager / Engineering Tech Lead / Engineering Team Lead.
